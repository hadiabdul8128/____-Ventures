# Boston Square Ventures — brand kit

Everything here is generated by `npm run brand:build` (`scripts/brand-kit.mjs`). Edit the script, not the files.

## The mark

A square. **B** in the top-left, **V** in the bottom-right. White on black is the primary version; black on white is for light backgrounds; the transparent versions go over photography or color. Letterforms are Google Sans Flex SemiBold converted to outlines, so nothing here depends on a font being installed.

Clear space: keep at least the stroke width (≈5.5% of the mark's width) clear on every side. Minimum size: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Don't recolor, rotate, add a drop shadow, or put the letters outside the square.

## Colors

| Role        | Hex       |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Black       | `#000000` |
| White       | `#ffffff` |
| Site text   | `#f4f4f2` |
| Gray        | `#8b8b86` |
| Dim gray    | `#5a5a56` |
| Hairline    | `#1c1c1c` |

No accent color. Hierarchy comes from size, weight, and white vs. gray.

## Type

**Google Sans Flex** (SIL Open Font License, on Google Fonts and Fontsource). Fallback: **Figtree**. Headlines at weight 500 with tight tracking (−0.03 to −0.04 em); body at 300–400; accents use the slant axis (oblique 10°) rather than a true italic.

## Voice

Lowercase sentences, short, a little irreverent, never cute. Tagline: **start at square one.**
